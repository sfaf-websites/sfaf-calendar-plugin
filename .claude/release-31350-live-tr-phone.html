Text registrants Send a text or WhatsApp message to everyone who chose one.

0 of 600 characters. By text, about 1 piece. Accented letters make each piece shorter.

What can I send by WhatsApp?

OK to send:

  • Changes to the time, date or location
  • Cancellations or postponements
  • What to bring, what to expect, accessibility or parking info
  • Check-in details or directions on the day

Not OK:

  • Promoting other events or programs
  • Donations or fundraising
  • Surveys, feedback or “rate this event”
  • Newsletters or general SFAF announcements
  • “Don’t miss,” “join us for,” or offers of any kind

If WhatsApp decides a message is promotional, it may block it, and future messages too.

Recipients

Sending to 3 people: 2 by text, 1 by WhatsApp.
