Calendar Users

Who can use the calendar, and who gets told about events. Two separate things, edited separately.

WordPress Users

Access level is what somebody may do in /caladmin. Teams are who gets notified about an event. Changing one never changes the other, and neither one changes anybody's WordPress role. A WordPress administrator always has full calendar access, whatever is listed here, so their access level is shown as fixed rather than offered as a control that would do nothing.
