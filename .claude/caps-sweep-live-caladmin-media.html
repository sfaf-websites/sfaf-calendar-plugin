Images

The pictures in the calendar folder. Tag one with a series to make it easy to find later. A picture showing its file name has no name yet; type one in the box under it.

Name is how you find the picture in a chooser. Short, and what somebody would search for: “Cycle To Zero”.

Alt text is read out instead of the picture to somebody who cannot see it, and it is what a search engine reads. Describe the picture, not the programme: “three cyclists on a coastal road”, not “Cycle To Zero”.

Series files the picture under a programme. A picture with no series is offered on no form, because the request forms show only the pictures tagged to the series somebody chose.
