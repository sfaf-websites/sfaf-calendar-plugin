CTZ @ Castro Street Fair

Come visit the Cycle to Zero booth at the famous and always fun and sunny Castro Street Fair. We’ll be set up right in front of Strut, SFAF’s community building and sexual health clinic.

Come chat and connect with staff about CTZ and how to get involved as a Cyclist, Crew, or Volunteer. And bring your friends – we’d love to meet them and answer any questions they have. We’ll have a special registration promotion happening!

See you there!

Queer Intimacies – Art Opening for Harry Stone

Test event HYBRID

Events held at Strut, including art openings, open mic nights, and other community events.

TransLife Drop-In

TransLife empowers and supports group members with whatever they are going through. We understand that life as a trans woman, trans man, or gender-fluid individual can be tough. Our group brings a blend of spirituality, friendship and community to give the support you need and the family you long for. Come join us.

Questions? Email Translife@sfaf.org or call 415-487-8090.

Group held at 940 Howard St.

Brothers Who Read

Join Black Brothers Esteem (BBE) for a weekly book group for Black men to read together, reflect, and engage in meaningful conversation through literature.

All are welcome and encouraged to join.

Virtual option available, contact bbe@sfaf.org for info.

Soul Sessions

Join HUES (Healing & Uniting Every Sista) for a twice-monthly gathering for Black women. We offer a warm and welcoming space to connect, unwind, and thrive, with engaging discussion, fun games, creative crafts, and shared experiences.

We foster joy, wellness, and community connection.

Virtual option available. Contact Ebony at egordon@sfaf.org for more info or to join virtually.

Aging Services: Virtual Check-In

Join us over Zoom as we check in with each other from the comfort of our own homes. Sometimes there is a topic of discussion, and sometimes it is a check-in to see how everyone is doing. Stay connected, and also find out what is happening in and around SFAF.

BBE Game & Movie Night

Join Black Brothers Esteem (BBE) once a month to play board games, watch movies, and play cards with the goal of not only winning but having fun.

Our Game Night is fun and friendly for casual board gamers, but also a great place to play the heavy cardboard. We will teach you new games, or you can bring your own and teach us.
